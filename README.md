# US-Navy-SH-60B-Helmet-Audio-Cord-Adapter
SH-60B Helmet Audio Cord Adapter: U-384/U to U-174/U

There are products available that allow people to use their military aviation helmets with their PCs such as the FlightSounds' Solo-M military headset adapter (https://flightsounds.com/products/solo-m-military).  These systems generally accept the U-174/U 4-conductor audio jack.  Unfortunately, SH-60B helmets used the U-384/U 5-conductor audio jack to plug in to the helicopter's communication system.  They also had a 27.5 volt DC amplifier on the microphone line.  These helmets will work with the FlightSounds system, but you have to do two things.  

First, you must bypass the amplifier.  This is a simple matter of unplugging the microphone's two-pin connector from the amplifier, unplugging amplifier's the two-pin connector from the base of the helmet, and then plugging in the microphone's two-pin connector to the base of the helmet.

Second, you have to create an adapter cable to change your helmet's 5-conductor audio jack (U-384/U) into a 4-conductor audio jack (U-174/U).  For this you will need a U-385/U receptacle (https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Amphenol-Nexus-Technologies/U-385-U?qs=NoqMqJKvPFIWB0bzhNX%252BGA%3D%3D) that you plug your helmet into.  You then wire that to a U-174/U jack (https://www.pilotshq.com/products/david-clark-electrical-connector-09226p-02) that will plug into your PC's headset adapter.
